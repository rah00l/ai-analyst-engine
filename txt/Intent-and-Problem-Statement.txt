## 1. Refined & Professional Description of the Payment Reconciliation Journey

### Payment Reconciliation – End‑to‑End Flow

The Payment Reconciliation process follows a well‑structured, multi‑step workflow designed to support accountants and analysts throughout the payment settlement lifecycle.

**Step 1: Payment File Upload (Upload Screen)**  
The process begins with uploading a payment file through the initial upload screen. Once uploaded, the file becomes visible along with its current processing status.

**Step 2: Merchant Aggregation View (Summary Screen)**  
After successful parsing, the system presents an aggregated view of payment data grouped by merchant. This screen serves as a high‑level summary, allowing users to review totals derived from the uploaded payment file.

**Step 3: Missing / Exception Identification (Transaction‑Level Screen)**  
The third screen displays transaction‑level details, highlighting missing entries, parsing errors, mismatches, or validation issues detected during processing. This screen is critical for accountant review and validation, as it exposes all discrepancies that must be resolved before reconciliation proceeds.

**Step 4: Tenancy Settlement (Interactive Adjustment Screen)**  
Once discrepancies are reviewed, the user moves to the Tenancy Settlement screen. This screen enables interactive settlement actions such as:

*   Adding, updating, or removing adjustments
*   Allocating excess amounts
*   Associating amounts with campaigns created for specific partners

The tenancy screen acts as the core settlement workspace where accountants finalize payment distribution.

**Step 5: Reconciliation Decision & Completion**  
At this stage, the user decides whether to:

*   Proceed with **full reconciliation**, or
*   Defer settlement until associated campaigns are created

Upon completing full reconciliation, the system returns the user to the upload screen with a confirmation that reconciliation has been successfully completed.

**Step 6: Reconciliation Summary (Final Aggregated View)**  
The final screen provides a consolidated summary of reconciled vs. unreconciled data for the payment file, offering a complete view of the reconciliation outcome.

This represents the complete Payment Reconciliation lifecycle.
